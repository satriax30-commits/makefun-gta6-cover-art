**Neon Coast Cover Studio** — an offline web app that turns any photo and a caption into GTA-style cover art: the multi-panel poster look with bold caption lettering and a Vice City sunset/neon colour grade, exported as a PNG to share.

- **Any photo:** upload your own photos (they never leave your machine) or try the four built-in samples. Click a panel to pick it, drag to position its photo, scroll or use the slider to zoom, and fill several panels with one photo or several.
- **Five layouts:** Hero + Three, Four Grid, Nine Grid, Triple Strip, and Full Bleed.
- **Five colour grades:** Sunset, Neon Nights, Vice Gold, Teal Dusk, and Mono Noir — each a canvas filter plus a tint gradient, with film grain and a vignette on every cover.
- **Lettering:** a big condensed title (Anton, SIL OFL) with a sunset gradient fill, a neon script subtitle (Pacifico, SIL OFL), and a five-star heat-meter badge.
- **Fast and easy:** pick a layout, a grade, and a size — Square 1080 × 1080, Story 1080 × 1350, or Wide 1920 × 1080 — then one click exports the PNG. Undo and redo included.

Everything is original: no Rockstar Games logo, wordmark, official artwork, character, or font. The app is plain static files under `app/` with no build step and no network request.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.
